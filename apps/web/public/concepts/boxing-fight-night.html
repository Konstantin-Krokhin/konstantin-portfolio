FAQ

Before you ask

I've never boxed. Can I still come?
Absolutely — most students start from zero. Your free trial session is an assessment: we check your fitness, teach the basic stance and punches, and build your plan from there.
What should I bring to the first session?
Just workout clothes and indoor shoes. Wraps and gloves are provided for the trial — if you continue, we'll advise what to buy.
Do you train kids?
Yes — ask about youth groups and 1-on-1 sessions for teenagers. Message to discuss the right format for your child's age.
Where do sessions take place?
Toronto — exact gym location shared when you book. Private sessions can be arranged at partner gyms.
Can I prepare for a competition?
Yes. Artem prepares amateur boxers for competition, including sparring strategy, conditioning blocks, and corner work on fight day.
