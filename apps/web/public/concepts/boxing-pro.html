FAQ

Before you ask.

I've never boxed. Can I still come? +
Absolutely — most students start from zero. Your free trial is an assessment: we check your fitness, teach the stance and basic punches, and build your plan from there.
What should I bring to the first session? +
Workout clothes and indoor shoes. Wraps and gloves are provided for the trial — if you continue, we'll advise what to buy.
Do you train teenagers? +
Yes. Share your child's age when booking and we'll recommend the right format — group or private.
Where do sessions take place? +
Toronto — the exact gym location is shared when you book your trial. Private sessions can be arranged at partner gyms.
Can you prepare me for competition? +
Yes — competition prep is a specialty: sparring strategy, conditioning blocks, and corner work on fight day.
