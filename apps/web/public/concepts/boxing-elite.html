Questions

Everything you're
wondering

I've never boxed. Is this for me?
Yes — most clients start from zero. Your complimentary trial is an assessment: we check your fitness, teach the fundamentals, and design your program from there.
What should I bring to the trial?
Workout clothes and indoor shoes. Wraps and gloves are provided for the trial; if you continue, we'll advise on what to buy.
Do you coach teenagers?
Yes. Share your child's age when you book and we'll recommend the right format — group or private.
Where are sessions held?
In Toronto — the gym location is shared when you book. Private sessions can be arranged at partner gyms.
I'm interested in competing. Can you prepare me?
Absolutely — competition preparation is a specialty: sparring strategy, conditioning blocks, and corner work on fight day.
